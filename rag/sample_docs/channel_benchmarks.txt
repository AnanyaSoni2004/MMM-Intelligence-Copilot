Channel Performance Benchmarks — Aryma Labs Research

Industry Benchmark ROI by Channel (2023-2024 averages across client portfolio):

1. Email Marketing
Average ROI: 7.5x - 10.2x
Why high: Low production cost, high intent audience (opted-in users)
Saturation: Typically reached at $30K-$50K/month. Beyond this, unsubscribe rates increase and incremental revenue flattens.
Best practice: Segment by purchase history and lifecycle stage. Automated flows (welcome, abandoned cart) consistently outperform batch sends.

2. Paid Search (Google, Bing)
Average ROI: 3.8x - 5.1x
Why high: High purchase intent at moment of search. Brand terms outperform non-brand.
Saturation: $150K-$280K/month for most mid-market brands. Search volume is finite — spend beyond available search volume yields zero marginal return.
Best practice: Bid on branded terms aggressively (competitors do). Non-brand campaigns require tight negative keyword management.

3. Paid Social (Facebook/Instagram)
Average ROI: 2.2x - 3.5x
Adstock half-life: 7-14 days (creative fatigue is a key driver)
Saturation: $200K-$350K/month. Audience saturation is the primary constraint.
Best practice: Rotate creatives every 2-3 weeks. Prospecting audiences require higher spend but build long-term incrementality.

4. YouTube / Online Video
Average ROI: 1.8x - 2.8x
Adstock half-life: 14-21 days (longer brand recall vs. direct response)
Saturation: $100K-$200K/month
Best practice: 15-second non-skippable ads for brand awareness, 6-second bumpers for frequency. Skippable TrueView for lower-funnel.

5. Linear TV
Average ROI: 1.5x - 2.2x
Adstock half-life: 14-28 days
Saturation: Broad reach medium, saturation is less of a concern at typical brand budgets
Best practice: Daypart selection matters significantly. Prime time vs. daytime can show 2x difference in ROI. GRPs, not just spend, determine impact.

6. Display / Programmatic
Average ROI: 1.1x - 1.8x
Lowest ROI in the mix, but plays an important role in retargeting
Saturation: Often reached quickly. Most value extracted in first $60K-$100K/month
Best practice: Use primarily for retargeting warm audiences. Prospecting display consistently underperforms other channels.

7. Influencer / Creator Marketing
Average ROI: 2.5x - 4.0x (high variance — dependent on influencer quality and category fit)
Adstock half-life: 21-30 days (content lives on social feeds longer than paid ads)
Saturation: Difficult to define — scales with number of creators rather than spend per creator
Best practice: Micro-influencers (10K-100K followers) consistently show higher engagement rates and better ROI than macro-influencers for most DTC brands.

8. Radio / Audio
Average ROI: 1.3x - 2.0x
Primarily relevant for local brands or commuter demographics
Saturation: Highly local-market dependent
Best practice: Morning and evening drive times (6-9am, 4-7pm) significantly outperform other dayparts.
